“Small actions. Responsible water use. Stronger communities.”

Clean water is a vital community resource. In public spaces and campus facilities, leaking taps, overflowing overhead tanks, and damaged outlets often go unnoticed or unaddressed. JalaRaksha gives community members a direct, accountable way to report observations, track review progress, and learn practical water conservation habits.

How JalaRaksha Works

1. Observe & Report

Notice a leaking pipe, dripping public valve, or overflowing storage tank? Record it with broad location details. No account required.

2. Track with Reference Code

Every submission generates a private, unique reference code (e.g. JR-DEMO0001A). Check status updates without compromising your privacy.

3. Verified Resolution

Administrators and student volunteers review reports, coordinate maintenance, and publish verified updates. No false impact metrics.

Help Assess Local Water Awareness

Take our quick 2-minute anonymous survey. Your answers help document real community conditions, conservation awareness, and rainwater harvesting practices.
